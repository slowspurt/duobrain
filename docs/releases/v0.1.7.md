# duobrain v0.1.7

Records are written to be found, and an agent finds them in one step. In the product repository, run:

```sh
npx duobrain@0.1.7 install
```

## Added

- **`find`.** One structured query (`--query` in the user's words, plus `--intent`, `--expand`,
  `--entities`, `--exclude`, `--kinds`, `--limit`, or `--file <json>`) looks through wiki notes,
  tickets and session handoffs along two channels at once:
  - **search** matches words with BM25F (title and keywords weigh most, Korean is compared in
    two-character pieces, English is lightly stemmed), then judges by rule: a superseded note drops
    and its replacement rises, evidence for an open or named ticket rises, a summary folds in its
    sources, and the list stops at the first steep score drop. At most a few cards come back, each
    with why it matched, plus `match: strong | weak | none`.
  - **browse** lists every other record as one short line (title, status, date, short abstract),
    newest first, at most 100, so the agent can pick by meaning what word matching missed.
    `--no-browse` omits it.

  No bodies are returned. Cards link notes and tickets both ways (`evidenceFor`, `evidence`).
- **`wiki-get --path wiki/<id>.md`** and **`ticket-get --ticket <id>`** open one record in full.
- **Search metadata on notes.** A wiki note may carry `abstract` (one or two sentences) and
  `keywords` (up to 12, in every language the team uses, with entities and the questions it
  answers). Notes without them stay valid; `note-add` reports `searchMetadata.missing`.
- **Wiki in the briefing.** `status --brief` shows the evidence notes of my open tickets (up to 5)
  and notes others recorded after my last handoff (up to 3), as cards without bodies.

## Changed

- `wiki-list` returns cards without bodies. `wiki-list --full` returns every note as before.
- The AI guide's lookup limit is now: briefing, one `find` judged across both channels, at most two
  opened records (in a sub-agent when available), then `unknown` and a ticket. Lookups are answered
  in four lines with a quoted line from the record.

## Compatibility

No new events and no migration. Engines on 0.1.6 ignore `abstract` and `keywords`, so partners can
update one at a time. `wiki-search` is unchanged.

## Upgrading

`node .duobrain/bin/duobrain.js update` moves a vendored copy to v0.1.7; the partner pulls.

## Verification

Unit tests cover the search metadata, both channels, Korean and English word forms, supersession,
ticket evidence, summary folding, the cutoff and `none`. A two-clone test drives `find`, `wiki-get`,
`ticket-get`, `wiki-list` and the briefing through the CLI.
